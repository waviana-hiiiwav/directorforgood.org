# HiiiWAV x A.P.P.

## Finalist Pitch Coaching, Demo Day Production & Media Capture

**Proposal for Cofounders**

---

## Page 1 — Overview & Strategic Value

HiiiWAV will serve as an **official presenting partner** for the A.P.P. Finalist Pitch Coaching and Demo Day preparation, delivering a focused, high-impact program designed to elevate already-strong teams into **polished, stage-ready performers**.

This engagement is built around the belief that Demo Day success is driven less by slides and more by **clarity of story, confidence of delivery, demo execution, and stage presence**. HiiiWAV’s role is to take finalist teams that are already operating at a high level and **build on that foundation**—tightening narrative, sharpening performance, and ensuring seamless execution on stage.

**Branding & positioning**

* The program will be **presented by HiiiWAV**, giving HiiiWAV visible branding across workshops, mentorship sessions, and Demo Day preparation.
* This positioning reinforces HiiiWAV as an **integral part of the accelerator’s production and outcomes**, strengthening credibility with mentors, founders, sponsors, and ecosystem partners.
* Presenting status also supports mentor recruitment by clearly signaling that HiiiWAV is leading pitch preparation and Demo Day readiness.

**Key principle**
The readiness of the finalist teams is critical. This program is designed for teams that:

* Already have a compelling product or company story
* Have existing pitch decks (not starting from zero)
* Are capable of executing feedback quickly

HiiiWAV’s work assumes strong baseline teams and focuses on **amplification, refinement, and performance excellence**.

---

## Page 2 — Scope of Work & Deliverables

### 1) Workshop-based Pitch & Performance Coaching

HiiiWAV will deliver a structured workshop series guiding finalist teams through:

* Narrative clarity and investor-ready story structure
* Confident verbal delivery and stage presence
* Demo flow, pacing, and narration
* Timing, transitions, and audience engagement

Workshops combine group instruction with targeted feedback to ensure consistency across teams while preserving each team’s unique voice.

---

### 2) Pitch Assets & Deck Support (Lightweight)

Each finalist team will receive:

* **1-page Pitch Blueprint** outlining story beats, timing, and call-to-action
* **Scripted 30-second opening** designed for memorized, high-impact delivery
* **Light pitch deck editing and refinement**

Decks are expected to already exist and be fundamentally strong. HiiiWAV will:

* Tighten structure and flow
* Improve clarity and emphasis
* Ensure alignment between spoken pitch, slides, and demo

This is **not a ground-up deck creation process**.

---

### 3) Demo Readiness & Rehearsal Support

* Demo choreography coaching (what to show, when, and how to narrate it)
* Contingency planning for technical or live-demo issues
* Full recorded run-throughs so teams can review, self-critique, and improve
* Final polish sessions focused on confidence, pacing, and cohesion

---

### 4) Video Capture & Media Coordination (Before / After)

HiiiWAV will coordinate and integrate **video recording as part of the program**, including:

* Recording early and late-stage pitch performances to create **before/after comparisons**
* Coordinating filming logistics in collaboration with CF’s video team
* Ensuring recordings serve two purposes:

  * **Learning tools** for founders to see and correct performance issues
  * **Promotional assets** showcasing growth, polish, and program impact

Final editing may be handled by CF or a designated video partner unless otherwise agreed.

---

### 5) Demo Day Stage & Production Integration

* Run-of-show and cue coordination
* Stage timing, transitions, and tech alignment
* On-site Demo Day support to ensure smooth execution and confident performances

---

### 6) Mentor & Volunteer Collaboration

* HiiiWAV will **identify and recruit potential mentors and volunteers** aligned with pitch coaching, storytelling, and demo readiness.
* Mentor sourcing will be a **collaborative effort with CF**, including shared outreach, introductions, and alignment on roles.
* This collaboration ensures mentor participation supports program goals while strengthening ecosystem relationships.
