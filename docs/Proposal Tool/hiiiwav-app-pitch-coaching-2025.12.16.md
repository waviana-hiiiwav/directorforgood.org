HiiiWAV x A.P.P. Finalist Pitch Coaching & Demo Day Production
Proposal for Cofounders (Dec 2025)

Page 1 — Executive Summary
HiiiWAV will prepare three A.P.P. finalist teams to deliver high-impact, stage-ready pitches at Demo Day through a focused coaching and production sprint. This engagement is designed to elevate each team’s story, performance, demo readiness, and stage execution—with a process that is lightweight, repeatable, and optimized for live delivery.
This proposal intentionally does not include pitch deck creation/editing. Based on the performance format, teams will not need a deck to succeed; we will prioritize verbal clarity, stagecraft, timing, and demo execution instead.
Primary outcomes
A consistent, high-quality Demo Day experience across all finalist teams
Clear, compelling pitches with confident delivery
“No surprises” show-flow: cues, tech checks, timing, and stage readiness
Reusable templates and a coaching approach that can be repeated each cohort

Page 2 — Scope of Work and Deliverables
What HiiiWAV will deliver (included)
1) Curriculum + coaching sprint (workshop-based)
A structured workshop series to move teams from raw pitch → stage-ready performance
Practical coaching on story, hook, structure, performance, and demo choreography
2) Team-level pitch assets (no deck)
For each finalist team:
1-page Pitch Blueprint (structure, beats, timing, CTA)
Scripted 30-second opener (memorized “cold open” designed for stage)
3) Demo readiness support
Demo flow coaching (what to show, in what order, and how to narrate it)
Live demo contingency planning (fallbacks, “if X fails, do Y”)
Lightweight demo cue plan (what happens when, who clicks, who speaks)
4) Rehearsal and run-through capture
One full run-through process per team (recorded) so teams can iterate and self-practice
Final notes and polish after review
5) Demo Day stage + technical integration
Run-of-show support: run order, cue sheets, stage timing, and coordination with production
On-site support for stage/demos to ensure smooth execution during the program and event
What is explicitly removed from scope
Pitch deck creation, slide design, or deck polish (not required for performance success)
Full sizzle production / post-production editing unless separately contracted

Page 3 — Options, Pricing, External Costs, and Sponsor Upside
Option 1 (Recommended): Four-workshop sprint
Flat fee: $25,000
Includes everything in the “Scope of Work and Deliverables” above, delivered as a four-workshop arc plus rehearsal and show integration.
Option 2: Three-workshop sprint
Flat fee: $20,000
A tighter version of the same program, compressing content into three workshops while preserving the essentials:
Pitch Blueprint + 30-second opener per team
Demo choreography + contingency planning
Run-through capture + final polish
Demo Day stage/tech integration

External costs are not included (and will not be covered by HiiiWAV)
Any external or pass-through costs must be paid directly by the partner (or reimbursed only with written pre-approval), including but not limited to:
Mentor/workshop leader honoraria (if requested)
Venue rental (if not using HiiiWAV space)
Production rentals, AV equipment, staging, specialty tech, or additional crew
Travel, meals, shipping, printing, or other hard costs

HiiiWAV option to add Workshop sponsor / audit seats (not present on Demo Day)
HiiiWAV would have the option to source a separate Workshop Sponsor / Auditor option:
A sponsor (or a small group of vetted founders) can audit the workshops only
They are not included on Demo Day and do not participate in finalist selection or judging
HiiiWAV retains 100% of sponsor/audit revenue (separate agreement; does not reduce the flat fee above)

Roles and Responsibilities (high level)
HiiiWAV: program design + delivery, coaching, rehearsal capture process, demo/stage integration

Finalists: pre-work completion, attendance, responsiveness, timely asset delivery
